How to read this table
Weight = share of your portfolio. Today = price change today. Gain/Loss = against your average buying price. 52-week position: red dot means near the yearly low, amber near the yearly high. Yield = yearly dividend ÷ price (green is 4% or more). P/E = price ÷ yearly earnings (lower is cheaper). Payout = share of profit paid as dividend. Signal shows the most important rule that applies.

Update my portfolio

Bought or sold shares? Added a stock to study? List the changes below. Everything else (sector, prices, ranges, yield) is filled in automatically. Your details are encrypted on this device before they leave it, then GitHub applies the change and rebuilds this page in about a minute.

Changes to send (one per line, you can edit). Examples: BUY HUBC 100 @ 188.5 · SELL OGDC 20 @ 301 · WATCH ADD LUCK MTL PSO · WATCH REMOVE SNGP

On the GitHub page that opens, just press the green “Submit new issue” button. You must be signed in to GitHub. Only the repository owner’s update is applied.

Prices come from the PSX data portal and may be delayed. Dividend and earnings figures are the latest available and can be out of date. Alerts are prompts to check, not investment advice.
