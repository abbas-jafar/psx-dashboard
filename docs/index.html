How to read this table
Weight = share of your portfolio. Today = price change today. Gain/Loss = against your average buying price. 52-week position: red dot means near the yearly low, amber near the yearly high. Yield = yearly dividend ÷ price (green is 4% or more). P/E = price ÷ yearly earnings (lower is cheaper). Payout = share of profit paid as dividend. Signal combines your rules, the price trend and (when switched on) the AI analyst. Tap a signal to see exactly why.

Add or remove a stock

Type a PSX symbol to add it (sector, price, ranges and yield are filled in automatically). To remove one, press Remove on its row. Then press “Encrypt and open on GitHub” in the update box just below.

Set watchlist triggers

Leave a box empty to keep it unchanged; type 0 to clear it. This adds a line to the update list below.

Update my portfolio

Bought or sold shares? Added a stock to study? List the changes below. Everything else (sector, prices, ranges, yield) is filled in automatically. Your details are encrypted on this device before they leave it, then GitHub applies the change and rebuilds this page in about a minute.

Changes to send (one per line, you can edit). Examples: WATCH SET LUCK entry=360 yield=5 pe=8 · BUY HUBC 100 @ 188.5 · SELL OGDC 20 @ 301 · WATCH ADD LUCK MTL PSO · WATCH REMOVE SNGP

On the GitHub page that opens, just press the green “Submit new issue” button. You must be signed in to GitHub. Only the repository owner’s update is applied.
